NUKEFIRE CLIENT 0.3.1-beta.62
Accessible Output, Audio, and Speech

Beta.62 promotes the accepted Beta.62b2 accessibility/audio/speech work on top of Beta.61.

Highlights
- Reader command parity and accessible output/review cleanup.
- Semantic combat, combat-context, group, and vitals Audio Cues.
- HP 75/50/25/10, mana 20/5, movement 50/5, and strict greater-than-50-percent catastrophic-hit earcons.
- Single-vital Reader actions and feedback-hygiene fixes.
- NukeFire.Speech 1 semantic speech policy: complete visible text/history remains intact while NukeFire Self-Voice can receive a cleaner semantic stream.
- CR AUDIO controls the existing bounded local semantic Audio Cue engine.
- CR TUTORIAL remains the short four-step Reader essentials path.
- CR TUTORIAL AUDIO is the optional practical workflow for Voice, Audio Cues, SPEECH, OUTPUT, exact-line review, Reader History, and safety/recovery.
- No arbitrary command execution, scripting bridge, filesystem/shell/network control, or hardcoded accessibility-key takeover is introduced.
