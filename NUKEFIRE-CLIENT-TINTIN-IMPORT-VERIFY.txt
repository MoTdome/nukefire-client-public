NukeFire Client beta.20 — TinTin++ Import Verification

1. Keep the client disconnected for the first test.
2. Open Preferences and locate Import TinTin++ Aliases and Actions.
3. Paste:

   #alias {k} {kill %1;kick}
   #alias {heal %1} {cast heal %1}
   #action {%1 tells you '%2'} {tell %1 I heard you.} {3}
   #action {bad} {#math {x} {1}}
   #nop #alias {ignored} {quit}

4. Select Analyze Paste.
5. Confirm:
   - 2 aliases and 2 actions found.
   - the first alias and ordinary action are Ready.
   - heal is Translated to alias name heal.
   - the #math action is Unsupported and cannot be selected.
   - the #nop alias is ignored.
6. Import Selected with Keep existing selected.
7. Confirm imported Actions show OFF with #action list.
8. Confirm alias k expands normally after connecting.
9. Enable the imported tell action explicitly and verify it triggers once.
10. Use Undo Last Import and confirm the imported definitions disappear.
11. Test a duplicate with Keep existing, then Replace existing.
12. Change the client command prefix and confirm #all routes are translated to it.
13. Paste a large narrowed export and verify Preferences remains responsive.
14. Confirm terminal output, Communications, Mapper, screen-reader controls,
    sessions, Actions, Gags, Variables, and Speedwalk remain unchanged.

15. On a large paste, click Import Selected and confirm the button immediately
    changes to Importing… before the final result appears.
16. Confirm the final result reports imported aliases, disabled actions,
    replacements, duplicates, and capacity skips rather than failing silently.

17. Immediately after a successful import, enter #alias and confirm each imported
    alias is listed without restarting the client.
18. Execute one imported alias and confirm its generated commands are sent in order.
19. Enter #action and confirm imported Actions are present and remain OFF.
20. Use Undo Last Import and confirm the same lists update immediately.
